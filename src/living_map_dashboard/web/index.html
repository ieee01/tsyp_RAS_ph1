The Living Map · Phase 1 · Mines & tunnels

Knowledge survives the robot.

The Writer explores and drops radio beacons that remember what, where and when. They relay each other's memories out of the mine. Whether the Writer is destroyed, stuck or comes home, the command post sends the right robots, briefed with everything through the gateway, and they update the map as they act.
